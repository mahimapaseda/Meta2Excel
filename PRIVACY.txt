Meta Receipts Lite — Privacy notice
===================================

This application runs entirely on your Windows PC.

- It does not send your PDFs, receipts, or exports to any server.
- It does not include telemetry, analytics, or automatic update checks.
- PDFs you open are read locally to extract Meta Ads receipt fields.
- Excel/CSV files you export are written only to paths you choose.
- Temporary files may be created under your Windows TEMP folder during ZIP export and are deleted afterward when possible.

Keep exported workbooks secure: they may contain account names, payment references, and campaign spend data.
